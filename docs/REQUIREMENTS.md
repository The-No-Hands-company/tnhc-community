# Product requirements

## Goals

Connect TNHC's projects with people interested in following, discussing, testing, building, promoting, or purchasing their work. Support enthusiasts and customers as well as technical contributors.

## Roles

- Visitor: permitted public project discovery if enabled by product decision.
- Member: profile, follow, discussion, permitted updates, opportunities and messaging.
- Project maintainer: manage assigned project information and official updates.
- Moderator: review reports and apply documented community actions.
- Administrator: manage role assignment and platform settings.

Roles grant explicit capabilities; moderator access does not automatically grant unrestricted access to private messages. Public registration versus invitations remains an open launch decision.

## Functional rules

Project records show ownership category (official TNHC, community, partner), stage, summary, tags, media, links, updates and opportunities. Members choose interests and follow specific projects. Official project announcements require maintainer permission. Ordinary member posts remain clearly attributed. Search supports tags and stages; lists support pagination.

Messages are private to authorised participants; blocking stops new contact and unwanted notifications. Reports create auditable cases; reporter identity is restricted. Notifications have preferences and lead to content the member can still access.

## Android behavior

Design for phones with back navigation, readable text scaling, accessible touch targets, useful loading/empty/error states, and recovery from interrupted requests. Preserve drafts where practical and avoid duplicate submissions on retry. Do not imply offline delivery when a message has not reached the server.

## Release requirements

Before beta, account recovery, deletion handling, moderation, data access tests, backups and a support route must work. Define measurable performance thresholds and supported Android versions during technical planning; no unmeasured performance guarantees are made here.
