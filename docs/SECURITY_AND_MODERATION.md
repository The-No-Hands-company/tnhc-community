# Security, privacy and moderation

## Required controls

Server-side access checks for every protected operation; secure session handling; protected secrets; rate limits for registration, messaging and uploads; media validation and limits; role-change audit trails. Backups and restoration must be tested before beta.

## Community controls

Publish community rules and provide report, block, moderation queue, review outcomes and an appeal/support route. Restrict who can see reports and take action. Record reasons for moderation decisions. Avoid unrestricted moderator access to private conversations; determine a limited reported-content review policy.

## Privacy decisions before beta

Inventory collected data and purposes; define profile visibility, message/report access, retention and deletion behavior; document notification settings and account deletion. State what happens to shared discussions when an account is deleted. Restrict analytics/logs to necessary operational data. Specific legal and store obligations must be checked when distribution, audience and jurisdictions are selected; this document is a development plan, not a compliance certification.

## Incident handling

Assign an operator; contain compromised access, preserve appropriate audit evidence, assess affected information, follow applicable notification duties, and record the corrective work with affected/fixed versions. Do not publish sensitive incident evidence in the public changelog.
